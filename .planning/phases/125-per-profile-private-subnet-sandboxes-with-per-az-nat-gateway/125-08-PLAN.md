<objective>
Wire placement through `km create` and `km budget add`: populate the new network fields at
all three `compiler.NetworkConfig` construction sites, add the fail-fast guard, resolve
placement once into `SandboxSubnets` ahead of the Phase 124 AZ sweep, retarget the sweep at
that resolved list, teach `RankAZs` to drop NAT-less AZs for private sandboxes, and record
the placement on the sandbox row.

Purpose: this is where the phase becomes real. The sweep already handles ICE
classify-and-retry, capacity recording and AZ ranking — it must be retargeted, never
forked. Resolving placement once, before the sweep, is what makes that possible.

Output: private sandboxes land on private subnets, rotate across AZs that actually have
NAT, and are recorded as private; public sandboxes behave exactly as in Phase 124.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-125-24 | Denial of Service | private sandbox launched into a NAT-less AZ | high | mitigate | An ICE rotation into an AZ with no NAT would produce a booted sandbox with zero egress and no obvious cause. Task 3's `RankAZs` filter removes those AZs from the sweep entirely for private sandboxes, and the non-nil-empty case errors with a specific message instead of an ambiguous empty ranking. |
| T-125-25 | Information Disclosure | placement resolved after the sweep instead of before | medium | mitigate | Resolving inside or after the sweep could leave the compiler emitting public subnet ids for a profile that asked for private, silently giving the sandbox a public IPv4. The single pre-sweep resolution point plus `EffectiveSandboxSubnets()`'s fallback (Plan 05) close both directions, and Tests 1 through 5 pin the AZ-to-subnet pairing through reorder and rotation. |
| T-125-26 | Elevation of Privilege | half-created sandbox on a rejected private create | medium | mitigate | The fail-fast guard runs before the compiler and before `CreateSandboxDir`, so a rejected create leaves no terragrunt artifacts and no partially-provisioned infrastructure. Test 4 asserts it. |
| T-125-27 | Repudiation | unrecorded placement | medium | mitigate | Without `NetworkPlacement` on the row, the Plan 06 NAT-disable guard and the Plan 07 doctor checks are blind. Written on both the local and `--remote` metadata literals, and guaranteed to survive lifecycle rewrites by Plan 04. |

ASVS level 1. T-125-24 is high severity and is MITIGATED by Task 3 — this plan does not ship without the NAT-AZ filter. No package-manager installs in this plan, so no package-legitimacy checkpoint applies.
