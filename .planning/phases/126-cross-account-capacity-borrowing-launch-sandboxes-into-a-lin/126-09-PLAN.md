<objective>
Add the four `km doctor` checks for cross-account links: link well-formed, launcher
assumable, artifacts grant present, and no orphaned instances in the linked account.

Purpose: REQ-126-DOCTOR. Enrollment is a multi-step, two-credential process; doctor is
what tells an operator that a half-finished enrollment is why their create is failing,
and it is the only automated way an orphaned linked-account instance becomes visible
from the home control plane.

Output: four checks registered in the existing doctor pipeline.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-126-47 | Denial of Service | invisible orphaned instance | high | mitigate | The orphan check compares linked-account instances against the home inventory and reports unmatched ones with enough identity to act on. A reachability failure is reported distinctly, so an unreachable link never reads as clean. |
| T-126-48 | Elevation of Privilege | silently widened artifacts grant | high | mitigate | The grant check inspects the statement's actions and warns on any write-shaped action, so a manual or accidental widening of the one cross-account grant is surfaced. |
| T-126-49 | Tampering | doctor deleting in-use resources | high | mitigate | All four checks are strictly read-only and are kept out of every deletion and sweep path; the acceptance criteria require the executor to verify that by grep. This repo has a recorded incident of doctor's non-dry-run sweep deleting in-use platform roles. |
| T-126-50 | Spoofing | assume check passing without contacting the target | medium | mitigate | The check forces credential resolution with a real read rather than merely constructing a lazy config, so a broken trust policy cannot report healthy. |
| T-126-51 | Information Disclosure | doctor output | low | mitigate | The checks print parameter paths, ARNs and instance identifiers; the external id value is never printed. |
</threat_model>
